# PNG для эффектов

Файлы: Assets/Resources/Fun/. Изготовлены встроенным image_gen. Импорт: прозрачность, не более 512 px, без mipmaps и сжатия. Текст и рамки штампов рисуются кодом в цвет игрока.

Общий промпт эффектов:

> Standalone game effect PNG cutout. {description} Genuine transparent background with no floor or backdrop, no cast shadow outside the object, no glow or haze. Center the single isolated object with small transparent margins. Natural realistic colors, readable at small sizes, soft upper-left lighting. No text, no other objects.

- `tomato.png`: One whole ripe red tomato with small green stem, slightly three-quarter view, photorealistic natural skin, compact round silhouette.
- `egg.png`: One whole unbroken chicken egg with natural off-white shell, three-quarter view, photorealistic.
- `tomato-splat.png`: One flattened realistic smashed red tomato splat seen directly from above: irregular red juice and pulp impact mark, a few seeds, ragged small radiating splashes. No whole tomato, no surface underneath.
- `egg-splat.png`: One realistic raw egg impact splat seen directly from above: yellow ruptured yolk, translucent egg white, three small broken off-white eggshell shards. Irregular compact outline, no plate, no whole egg, no surface underneath.
